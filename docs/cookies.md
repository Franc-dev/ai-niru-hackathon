# Cookie Policy - Elevana

**Last Updated: March 2026**

## What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you use a website. They help the website remember your preferences and provide a better experience.

## How Elevana Uses Cookies
We use cookies for the following purposes:
- **Session Management:** To keep track of your current chat session.
- **Language Preferences:** To remember if you prefer Swahili or English.
- **Voice Settings:** To remember your preferred voice and playback settings.

## Managing Cookies
You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect some features of Elevana, such as maintaining chat history within a session.
