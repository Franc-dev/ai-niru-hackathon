# Privacy Policy - AI NirU

**Last Updated: March 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect
ELEVANA AI is designed with privacy in consideration and in mind, ensuring user data protection. 
We collect:
- **Chat/Voice Transcripts:** Temporarily processed to provide mental health support.
- **Usage Metadata:** Anonymized data to improve the model's accuracy (e.g., language, category).

## 2. How We Use Information
- To provide empathetic, bilingual mental health support.
- To detect and respond to mental health crises (e.g., self-harm).
- To improve our RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) knowledge base.
- To personalize recommendations and guidance based on individual mental health needs.

## 3. Data Storage
- Conversations are stored locally or in our secure database to provide a seamless user experience.
- No personally identifiable information (PII) is required to use the basic features of ELEVANA AI.

## 4. Voice Data
Voice data is processed in real-time. We do not store raw audio recordings; only the transcribed text is used for generating responses.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We use:
- **Pinecone:** For vector search.
- **ElevenLabs:** For high-quality voice synthesis.
- **Anthropic (Claude):** For real-time voice agent reasoning.

---
*Note ELEVANA AI is a hackathon prototype and not a replacement for professional therapy or medical advice.*
